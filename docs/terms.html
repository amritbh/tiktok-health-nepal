TikTok Health Nepal is an educational content creation tool that generates public health awareness videos for the general public in Nepal. The application uses AI to create animated health education content in Nepali language.

2. Purpose

This application is designed solely for creating and publishing health education content on TikTok. All content focuses on general public health tips and awareness using simple Do and Don't messaging.
